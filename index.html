01 · About

Electrical Engineer specializing in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Science and Technology at the University of Milano-Bicocca. My work focuses on understanding and modeling signals in different forms, from physiological signals such as ECG and EEG to images and sensor data, transforming them into intelligent systems for real-world applications.

My experience spans medical imaging and biosignal analysis, including skin lesion classification, tumor cell analysis, ECG arrhythmia detection, and EEG state classification, as well as computer vision tasks such as image classification, object detection, segmentation, and feature matching. Two and a half years at Bank of Abyssinia strengthened my foundation in production reliability, supporting ATM, ITM, and POS systems used by real customers.
